Data notes & methodology

This map displays 433 supplied polygons. The original grid spacing is 1,000 m in the source projected coordinates; displayed polygons are clipped to the city boundary. A grid cell is not a census tract or a named neighborhood.

Calculation basis requires confirmation. The team reports a full 1 km² denominator. However, the supplied canopy_pct and canopy_pct_final fields differ: grid 16 reports 0.456% and 53.83%, respectively. This version displays the final field without recalculation. Do not interpret it as a verified whole-cell or clipped-area measure until the original calculation is confirmed.

Five cells report 0% while canopymean is null: 323, 324, 350, 351, 586. They retain their reported values and carry hatching. Zero canopy and unavailable raster data have not yet been distinguished. Comparisons involving these cells carry a verification note.

Coverage is not a direct measure of shade experienced by residents. Data year and raster source metadata have not been supplied in the exported files. Grid IDs and row/column indices come directly from the export.
